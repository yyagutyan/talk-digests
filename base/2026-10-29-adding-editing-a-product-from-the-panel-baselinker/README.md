# Adding / editing a product from the panel | BaseLinker Academy

*How to add and edit products, with variants, in BaseLinker's built-in product storage, and which tabs hold which data.*

Source: [Adding / editing a product from the panel | BaseLinker Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5i7wkwgiljs) · Base · 7 min

## TL;DR

BaseLinker has its own internal product storage, and products in it must be complete before they can be handled further in the system. Adding a product takes a few required fields (name, inventory, category, producer), while SKU, EAN and weight are optional. The product editor is split into tabs for sales and storage, descriptions, marketplace parameters, photos, variants, and per-channel overrides. Filling these tabs properly is what makes a product ready for listing and fulfilment across channels.

## Key takeaways

- Add a new product with the green button in the product list, setting its name, inventory, category and producer.
- SKU, EAN and weight are optional when creating a product, but they can be filled in later.
- Unfinished products save automatically as drafts, and the draft filter lists them so you can finish them later.
- Custom fields are added the same way as other product data and are then filled in for each product individually.
- The Sales and storage tab holds prices per price group, VAT rate, warehouse stock and location, and suppliers.
- The Parameters tab holds the attributes each sales channel requires before it will list the product.
- Variants each get their own SKU and EAN, and the main product shows the combined stock of all its variants.
- Channel data lets you override product details for a specific marketplace, such as using a different eBay-specific product name.

## Creating and editing products in the panel

[▶ 00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5i7wkwgiljs&t=0s)

Add products with the green button in the product list. Set the name, inventory, category and producer; SKU, EAN and weight are optional. Drafts save automatically and can be found with the draft filter.

Custom fields are added the same way and filled in per product.

Product tabs cover:

- **Sales and storage:** prices per price group, VAT, warehouse stock and location, suppliers
- **Descriptions:** product name, main and marketplace-template descriptions
- **Parameters:** attributes each channel needs to list the product
- **Photos:** upload from a computer or by link
- **Variants:** own SKU and EAN; the main product shows combined stock
- **Channel data:** per-marketplace overrides, such as an eBay-specific name
