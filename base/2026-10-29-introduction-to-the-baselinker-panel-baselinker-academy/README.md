## TL;DR

Managing an online business across several tools means knowing where each function sits in the software. The BaseLinker panel handles this with a left-hand menu of five sections: Order manager, Products, Integrations, Help and contact, and Marketplace. Quick links, notifications, and the profile menu, which includes logout, are in the top-right corner, alongside a quick-access pop-up and the order finder. Knowing this layout lets you find orders, product data, integrations, and support without searching.

## Key takeaways

- The BaseLinker panel's left-hand menu has five sections: Order manager, Products, Integrations, Help and contact, and Marketplace.
- Use the Order manager section to process orders.
- The Products section is BaseLinker's internal warehouse, where your product database is managed.
- The Integrations section lists your integrations, lets you change their settings, and is where you add new ones.
- Use Help and contact to check system status and open support tickets.
- The Marketplace section shows the sales channels you have connected.
- The top-right corner holds quick links, notifications, and the profile menu, where you log out.
- A quick-access pop-up and the order finder sit next to the top-right controls.

The left-hand menu has five sections:

- **Order manager**: order processing.
- **Products**: the internal BaseLinker warehouse.
- **Integrations**: the integration list, their settings, and adding new ones.
- **Help and contact**: system status checks and support tickets.
- **Marketplace**: connected sales channels.

The top right holds quick links, notifications, and the profile menu, where you log out. A quick-access pop-up and the order finder sit beside them.
